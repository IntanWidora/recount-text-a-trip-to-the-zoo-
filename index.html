What is a Recount Text?

A Recount Text is a text that tells the reader about one story, action, or activity that happened in the past.

Main Purpose of Recount Text

To retell past events or personal experiences in chronological order (time sequence) for the purpose of informing or entertaining the audience.

Generic Structure of Recount Text:

Part 1
1. Orientation

Introduces WHO was involved, WHERE it happened, and WHEN it took place.

Part 2
2. Events

Tells what happened in chronological sequence (First, Next, Then, After that, Finally).

Part 3
3. Re-orientation

Summary or personal feeling/opinion about the experience (e.g. "We were happy!").

Key Language Features:
  • Uses Simple Past Tense (e.g., visited, saw, bought, took).
  • Uses action verbs (e.g., walked, fed, roared).
  • Uses time connectives (e.g., First, Then, After that, Finally).

Example Recount Text

A Wonderful Day at Safari Zoo

Orientation Last Sunday, my family and I visited Ragunan Zoo in Jakarta. We went there by car early in the morning because we wanted to see all the animals.
Events

First, we bought our entrance tickets and walked toward the primate area. We saw clever monkeys swinging on ropes.

Next, we went to the elephant habitat. My brother fed the gentle giants with fresh carrots and bananas.

After that, we watched a fierce lion that roared loudly inside its enclosure. I took many beautiful photos with my camera.

Re-orientation Finally, we bought ice cream and headed back home at 4 PM. It was an unforgettable experience and we felt super happy!
